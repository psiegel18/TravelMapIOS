Travel Mapping iOS App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Overview

Travel Mapping is an iOS app for browsing and tracking road and rail travel data from travelmapping.net. This privacy policy describes what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Location Data

  • When used: Only when you explicitly start recording a Road Trip via the Road Trips tab, tap the location button on a map view, or choose "Current Location" in the Route Planner.
  • How it's used: To record GPS coordinates of routes you drive so they can be matched to TravelMapping route segments.
  • Where it's stored: Recorded trips are stored locally on your device and, if you have iCloud Drive enabled, in your personal iCloud container. Recorded trips and GPS traces are never sent to any third-party server.
  • Route Planner: If you choose "Current Location", that location is sent to Apple Maps (MapKit) to calculate directions and to rank nearby address suggestions, the same way an address you type is. Addresses you type are sent to Apple Maps for suggestions and directions. The planner then asks travelmapping.net for the highway segments in the area the route covers. Sharing a route (and any Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze link in it) happens only when you choose to share, with the app you pick. Tapping Navigate passes the start and end points to the navigation app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze).
  • When it stops: GPS tracking stops when you tap "Stop Road Trip" or close the recording session.

User Preferences

  • Favorited users, primary username, map display settings, and unit preferences are stored on your device.
  • These preferences may sync across your Apple devices via iCloud Key-Value Storage if you have iCloud enabled.

Network Requests

The app fetches publicly available data from:

  • travelmapping.net — route data, map segments, traveler statistics, and the nightly update log for your username (List checkup)
  • tmrail.teresco.org — rail route data and its nightly update log for your username
  • github.com/TravelMapping/UserData — user travel list files and the list of TravelMapping usernames (Travelers tab and username suggestions)
  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — map tiles, Route Planner directions, and address suggestions

No personal information is transmitted to us. The app does not have its own user accounts.

Optional GitHub Sign-In

Signing in is optional: without it, the app can still build your updated .list or .rlist file for you to copy into GitHub yourself. If you choose to sign in with GitHub (Settings > GitHub) so the app can open those pull requests for you:

  • Sign-in uses GitHub's device authorization flow — you approve access on github.com; your GitHub password never passes through the app.
  • The app requests the public_repo scope only, and uses it solely on your fork of TravelMapping/UserData and the pull requests the app opens: to fork the repository, commit your updated list file to a new branch, open a pull request, add later updates to a pull request that is still open (bringing its branch up to date with TravelMapping first, and keeping its title and description current), and look up whether its pull requests are open, merged, or closed.
  • Under Settings > GitHub > Finished PRs you choose what happens after a pull request is finished: the app can delete the branches it created on your fork once their pull request is merged or closed, and bring your fork's main branch up to date with TravelMapping — only when that branch has no changes of its own. You can limit this to deleting branches, or turn it off. The app never touches branches it didn't create or ones with an open pull request.
  • The access token is stored in this device's Keychain and sent only to api.github.com. It stays on that device unless you turn on "Stay Signed In on My Other Devices", which syncs it end-to-end encrypted through your iCloud Keychain. Signing out deletes it (from all your devices, if syncing is on).
  • Pull requests, and the route segments they add, are public on GitHub like any other contribution to TravelMapping/UserData.

Optional AI Assistant (Your Own API Key)

You can connect your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), xAI (Grok), or another OpenAI-compatible service to get suggested fixes for lines of your list file. This is off unless you add a key and give permission:

  • Nothing is sent to an AI provider until you explicitly allow it for that provider in the app, and only when you ask for a suggestion.
  • What is sent: the lines of your list file that TravelMapping's nightly update flagged, TravelMapping's notes about them, and public TravelMapping route data (waypoint names and coordinates) for those routes.
  • What is never sent: your recorded GPS trips or location, the rest of your list file, your email, your GitHub account, or other keys.
  • Requests go directly from your device to the provider you chose, under your own account and that provider's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your key, your requests, or the AI's answers.
  • Your key is stored in your device's Keychain. It stays on that device unless you turn on "Use on My Other Devices", which syncs it end-to-end encrypted through your iCloud Keychain. AI suggestions never change your list file on their own — you review every change before sending it.
  • A log of each request (date, provider, model, a description of what was sent, and the token counts the provider reported) is kept on your device. You can turn AI off, revoke permission, clear the log, or remove your key at any time in Settings > AI Assistant (removing a synced key removes it from all your devices). AI request errors are not sent to our crash reporting.

Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

The app uses Sentry to collect anonymous diagnostic data that helps us identify and fix bugs and performance issues. Data collected may include:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Stack trace information (which code was executing when an error occurred)
  • Performance metrics (CPU profiles, app hang detection, slow screen transitions)
  • Screenshots and view hierarchy captured at the moment of an error
  • Session replay video (a visual recording of the few seconds leading up to an error) for a sampled subset of sessions
  • Breadcrumbs and structured logs of app events (trip lifecycle, navigation, settings changes) — no trip coordinates, note content, or personal data
  • General device state (memory usage, battery level, connectivity)

Diagnostic data is not used for advertising, tracking, or analytics. It is used solely to improve app functionality. Session replays exclude any content inside AVFoundation views, system photo pickers, and camera views by iOS design. Diagnostic data is not linked to your identity: your TravelMapping username and GitHub login are removed from error reports, traces, breadcrumbs, and logs, and session replays and error screenshots hide your username wherever the app shows it as yours, your GitHub account, API keys, and the Route Planner's addresses, map, and directions. You can report bugs directly via the shake gesture or Settings > Report a Bug. Reports and feedback are anonymous: the app doesn't ask for your name or email, so we can't reply to them directly.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (email, phone, real name) with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising or user-tracking SDKs.
  • We do not share your road trip data or location history.

Data Storage

  • Device: User preferences, favorites, recent users, cached data, and road trip recordings.
  • iCloud (optional): Favorites, user preferences, road trip files, the list update pull requests the app opened (number and the segments they add), and segments you marked "Didn't Travel" sync across your Apple devices if enabled.
  • Keychain: Your GitHub token and AI API key, if you add them (iCloud Keychain sync is optional).
  • External servers: None run by the developer. The app reads public data from the sources listed above and, only if you turn them on, talks to GitHub and the AI provider you chose.

Background Location

The app uses background location updates only when actively recording a road trip. The blue status bar indicator will be visible during recording. Background tracking stops when the trip ends.

Your Choices

  • iCloud sync: Can be toggled on/off in Settings.
  • Location: Required only for road trip tracking. You can deny location permission and still use the rest of the app.
  • Cache: Can be cleared at any time from Settings.
  • Recent users: Can be cleared at any time.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
